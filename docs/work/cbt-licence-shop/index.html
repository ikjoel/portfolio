CBT Licence Shop

The shop sells licences for the exam software. Buyers are exam-centre owners paying from a phone. I designed the storefront and built payment, delivery and the owner’s admin.

(01)

Problem

  • Buyers hesitate to pay online, and wonder after paying whether it worked.
  • They also ask, before buying, which server computer they need.
  • The owner must be certain that no paid order is ever lost.
This led to· Abandoned checkouts
· Support questions about hardware
· Fear of missing orders

(02)

Solution

A pricing page that helps people decide, a requirements page that answers the hardware question, server-side payment verification, and automatic delivery of the signed licence by email and download.

(03)

Key design decisions

1.

Pricing that helps people decide

  • One main action and one quiet alternative.
  • A highlighted plan, and sample prices clearly marked as samples in the portfolio.
2.

Answer the hardware question before it is asked

  • A single table: how many candidates at once, and which computer you need.
  • The three things every size needs: Windows 10 or 11, a UPS and a wired Gigabit link.
3.

A payment that is never lost

  • Every payment verified on the server, plus webhook handling.
  • A scheduled reconciliation job re-checks every paid order.
  • The signed licence is delivered by email and by download.
4.

An owner who can see what needs a look

  • Orders list with payment and delivery state.
  • The result of the nightly reconciliation shown on the dashboard.
  • An agent portal for resellers.

(04)

Outcome

  • Live and selling.
  • 30 automated tests.
  • Lost webhooks cannot lose an order.

(05)

My role

  • Storefront design and implementation.
  • Payment verification, webhooks, reconciliation and email delivery.
  • Owner admin and agent portal.

(06)

Key takeaway

Trust is built by answering the next question before the user asks it.
