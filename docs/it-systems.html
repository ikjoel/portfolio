Operations practice

The habits that keep a small team’s systems safe.

  • Change one thing at a time, keep a way back, and test the way back.
  • Take a backup before every change; test a restore before you need it.
  • Prefer fixing the cause to restarting the symptom, and write down what the cause was.
  • Start a new defence in monitoring-only mode before turning it on.
  • Explain every problem in words the user understands.
  • Keep runbooks short enough to use at 2 a.m.
